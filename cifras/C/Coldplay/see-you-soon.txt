Note: For tuning just tune to Jimmy Page tuning, but for the 2nd string
      tune it to b instead of a. Then capo at the 2nd fret. You don't
      really have to see the chart all the time, just know when to
      change your chord & strum it by hearing. It's not really
      strumming nor is it plucing. What is it? Just do it by hearing.
