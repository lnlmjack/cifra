Symbols and other stuffs.
p  - pulloff       h - hammeron    A.H. - artificial harmonics
fb - full bend    hb - half bend     qb - quarter bend
s  - slide         ~ - vibrato        r - release bend

I wrote slash in a long drawn out form to separate lines that might be pushed
together if you print directly from the page.  Also check out my copy of
November Rain.  I got this directly from the GN'R Lies book so don't complain
to me saying it's wrong. Have a nice day. Any questions?
