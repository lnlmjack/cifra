What a pleasure it is to be able to play this song on an acoustic guitar

I'm not the Off-The-Record man  I've transcribed the way I'm playing it
but unless you really start the instrument (& then what are you doin'g here?
Get back to Let it be!) I don't think that it's a good idea to play the tune
in exactly the same way  My advice: Take a look at verse 1  it's all over
the same part except for the 2 last bars  So you can only learn verse 1 &
links to play the thing like that:
