Pink Floyd - Interstellar Overdrive

This is the main guitar riff which appears as
the intro and in various places thoughout
the song.

E5 (heavily palm muted) repeated using downstrokes
then

A A Ab A Ab
G G Gb G Gb
F F F F F F G  G G E5

B B Bb B Bb
A A Ab A Ab
G G G G G G G  A A  E5

use barre chords.

then everything goes a bit mad!!!

if you can't get the rhythm correct try thinking
of the theme tune to "Steptoe and son" because

it's almost the same..

----------------- Acordes -----------------
A = X 0 2 2 2 0
Ab = 4 3 1 1 1 4
B = X 2 4 4 4 2
Bb = X 1 3 3 3 1
E5 = 0 2 2 X X X
F = 1 3 3 2 1 1
G = 3 2 0 0 0 3
Gb = 2 4 4 3 2 2
