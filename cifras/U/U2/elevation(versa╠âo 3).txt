U2 - Elevation

Note: The Edge seems to be using a lot of different distortion effects through most of the song.  I've tried to match them as best as I can:  Guitar #1 requires the gain and bass buttons on your amp to be turned all the way up (10), and the treble button to be turned all the way down.  Guitar #2 requires the gain, treble, and bass to all be turned up to 10.
