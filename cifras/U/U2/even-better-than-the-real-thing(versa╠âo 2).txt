 **INTRO**

 D5 - x577xx

 At the beginning of the song The Edge plays a D5 chord that he modifies with
 his DigiTech "Whammy". So he can create this special sound.
 If you haven't got a "Whammy" you can create a similar sound
 with a small trick.
 Play the Ab and the Db string together and repeat this 2 times,
 then play the Gb string and the other 2 strings alternately.

 So, that's it, not very difficult.
